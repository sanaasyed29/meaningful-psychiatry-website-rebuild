Meaningful Psychiatry wants everyone to be able to learn about our care and book an appointment, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification, or other assistive technology.

## What we do

- We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.
- Our pages work with keyboard navigation and include a "Skip to content" link.
- Images include text descriptions, and our colors are chosen for readable contrast.
- Pages adjust to phones, tablets, and computers, and to larger text sizes.
- Animations pause for visitors who have "reduce motion" turned on in their device settings.

## Need help?

If any part of this website is hard to use, or you need information in a different format, please contact us and we'll help you directly:

- **Call or text:** 425-272-5931
- **Email:** admin@meaningfulpsychiatry.com

Some parts of the booking process happen on Osmind, our patient platform. If you have trouble booking online, call or text us and we'll schedule your appointment for you.
