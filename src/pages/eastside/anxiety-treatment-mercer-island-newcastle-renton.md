Persistent worry, racing thoughts, and physical symptoms like heart palpitations or restlessness can make everyday life feel exhausting. If anxiety is impacting your work, relationships, or overall well-being, finding structured, evidence-based care is essential.

## Recognizing the symptoms of anxiety

Anxiety shows up differently for everyone, often combining mental, emotional, and physical symptoms:

- **Generalized anxiety:** Excessive, uncontrollable worry about health, finances, or family routines—whether you're navigating corporate pressures or managing a busy household in Issaquah and Newcastle.
- **Panic and somatic symptoms:** Sudden episodes of intense fear, chest tightness, shortness of breath, or stomach distress.
- **Social and performance anxiety:** Heightened fear of judgment, leading to avoidance of social gatherings or professional meetings.

## How Meaningful Psychiatry approaches anxiety care

Our providers offer an integrative, whole-person approach that goes beyond symptom suppression. Care plans focus on addressing root causes and restoring long-term balance through:

- **Tailored medication management:** Using evidence-based psychiatric medications when indicated, with careful consideration of past responses and side effects.
- **Psychotherapy:** Incorporating cognitive and mindfulness-based approaches to help reframe anxious thought patterns.
- **Mind-body and somatic support:** Exploring lifestyle factors, sleep hygiene, and somatic strategies to regulate the nervous system.
- **Culturally sensitive care:** Honoring your personal values, background, and spiritual perspectives throughout treatment.
