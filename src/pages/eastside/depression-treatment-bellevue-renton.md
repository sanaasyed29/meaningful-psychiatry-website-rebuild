Depression is more than feeling sad—it can feel like a heavy cloud affecting your energy, motivation, sleep, and self-worth. When low mood begins interfering with daily life, professional psychiatric support offers a clear path toward healing and emotional resilience.

## Signs you may be experiencing depression

Symptoms of depression can range from mild to severe and often include:

- Persistent feelings of sadness, emptiness, or hopelessness
- Loss of interest or pleasure in activities you once enjoyed
- Low energy, chronic fatigue, or changes in sleep and appetite
- Difficulty concentrating or making decisions, a common challenge for professionals and students across the I-90 corridor from Mercer Island to Issaquah
- Feelings of worthlessness or excessive guilt

## Comprehensive care with our providers

Treatment at Meaningful Psychiatry centers on seeing you as a whole person—not just a set of symptoms. Our providers combine clinical expertise with compassionate, non-judgmental guidance:

1. **Thorough diagnostic evaluation:** Identifying underlying medical, psychological, or situational contributors to your mood.
2. **Individualized medication plans:** Finding the right psychiatric support tailored to your unique biology and lifestyle goals.
3. **Integrative psychotherapy:** Exploring personal identity, relationship dynamics, and life transitions in a supportive environment.
4. **Holistic wellness support:** Incorporating physical health, social supports, and value-based strategies to build sustainable resilience.
