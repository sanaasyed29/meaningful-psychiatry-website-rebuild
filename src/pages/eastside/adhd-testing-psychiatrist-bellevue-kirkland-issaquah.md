## Recognizing ADHD symptoms in children, teens, and adults

Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) presents differently across age groups and individuals:

- **Inattention and executive functioning:** Difficulty keeping track of tasks, frequent disorganization, missing details, or struggling to follow multi-step directions at school or work.
- **Hyperactivity and impulsivity:** Restlessness, excessive talking, squirming, interrupting others, or making quick decisions without weighing consequences.
- **Emotional regulation and burnout:** Struggling with overwhelm, task initiation, or stress management when balancing academic demands or high-paced tech careers in Bellevue and Kirkland.

## The integrative evaluation process at Meaningful Psychiatry

Our psychiatric providers look beyond surface-level symptoms to consider the full picture of health—including physical, emotional, and cultural contexts.

1. **In-depth clinical assessment:** Gathering comprehensive developmental, academic, and health histories alongside personal goals.
2. **Standardized clinical rating scales:** Using validated tools completed by patients, parents, or educators where appropriate.
3. **Differential diagnosis:** Evaluating co-occurring conditions such as anxiety, depression, or sleep disturbances to ensure an accurate diagnosis.
4. **Tailored treatment planning:** Creating a holistic plan that may include evidence-based medication management, individual therapy, executive functioning strategies, and school or work accommodations (such as 504 Plans or IEPs).
