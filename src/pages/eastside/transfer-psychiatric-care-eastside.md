Switching mental health providers—whether you are relocating to Bellevue or Issaquah, looking for an in-network or private-pay practice like Meaningful Psychiatry, or seeking a provider who fits your schedule in Newcastle or Renton—can feel overwhelming. However, transferring your care does not have to interrupt your treatment or medication routine.

By following a few simple steps, you can ensure a seamless transition for medication management and ongoing psychotherapy, all through convenient virtual visits across Washington State.

## Step 1: Secure your records and sign an authorization

Your new psychiatric provider needs a comprehensive picture of your medical history, diagnostic background, and previous medication responses.

- **Request a Release of Information (ROI):** Contact your previous clinic and submit an ROI allowing them to share records with Meaningful Psychiatry.
- **Gather key documents:** Keep copies of recent psychiatric evaluations, psychological or ADHD assessment reports, and a current list of all prescriptions.

## Step 2: Establish a medication bridge

To avoid any interruption in daily psychiatric medications, plan ahead before your current supply runs low.

- **Ask for a transitional refill:** Request a 30- to 90-day refill from your current prescriber to bridge the gap until your initial intake evaluation.
- **Schedule early:** New patient evaluations require dedicated time for a thorough 60-minute intake, so book your appointment well in advance.

## Step 3: Prepare for your integrative intake session

At Meaningful Psychiatry, care goes beyond a checklist—it takes a holistic view of your mind, body, lifestyle, and personal values. To make the most of your first appointment:

- **Summarize treatment history:** Bring details on past medications (dosages, side effects, and effectiveness) and therapy approaches you've tried.
- **Outline your goals:** Note what you hope to achieve, whether that involves fine-tuning medication, exploring culturally responsive therapy, or integrating lifestyle and somatic wellness supports.
