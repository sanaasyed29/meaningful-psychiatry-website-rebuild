## Understanding your Ambetter costs

Ambetter plans come in different **metal tiers** (Bronze, Silver, Gold), and each tier handles costs differently:

- **Bronze plans** usually have lower monthly premiums but higher deductibles, so you may pay more per visit until your deductible is met.
- **Silver and Gold plans** usually have higher premiums but lower out-of-pocket costs per visit.
- **HSA-eligible plans:** If your Ambetter plan is HSA-eligible, you can usually use your HSA card for visit costs.

When you call Ambetter, ask:

1. Is Francis Agbotse, PMHNP in-network for my plan?
2. What do I pay for an outpatient mental health visit, and does it count toward my deductible?
3. Are telehealth visits covered the same as in-person visits?
4. Do I need a referral or prior authorization?
