## Understanding ADHD — Beyond the Stereotypes

ADHD is widely discussed today, but often misunderstood. Many people think ADHD is simply about distraction or hyperactivity. In reality, it’s a neurological condition that genuinely impacts a person’s ability to manage daily tasks, responsibilities, and emotions.

> **“People often misunderstand ADHD as a lack of effort. The symptoms truly impact daily life, and our expectations of the mammalian brain need to be more realistic.” — Dr. Qureshi**

This is why Dr. Qureshi emphasizes **rest as part of treatment**, not just medication, and proactively works to **counteract side effects** so patients can function comfortably.

## What Makes Dr. Qureshi’s ADHD Treatment Approach Unique

Unlike many psychiatric practices that focus narrowly on symptoms or prescriptions, Dr. Qureshi’s approach is holistic, slow, and steady — designed to help patients reclaim their lives safely and with confidence.

### 1. Helping Patients Rebuild Executive Function

ADHD often erodes a person’s belief in their own abilities.

> **“My goal is to help patients regain confidence in their capability to do daily functions and reclaim their executive function.”**

Her work is as much about empowerment as it is about treatment.

### 2. A Step-Wise, Safety-Focused Medication Process

ADHD medication can be transformative — but only when handled thoughtfully.

> **“I never overshoot treatment. I take a step-wise approach, especially for patients with cardiac or seizure history. And I give a lot of psychoeducation so people know exactly where we are and why.”**

This careful process minimizes anxiety, over-activation, increased heart rate, or feeling “too wired.”

### 3. Psychotherapy at Every Appointment

Many clinics separate therapy and medication management.

Dr. Qureshi doesn’t.

> **“Every single appointment involves psychotherapy. I want to know the top concern on a patient’s mind and how their symptoms are affecting their life.”**

Medication alone cannot rebuild executive function — but medication with therapy can.

### 4. A Diagnostic Process That Goes Beyond Trends

With ADHD content everywhere, many people misdiagnose themselves or feel confused.

> **“I cut through popular ADHD sentiments with real diagnostic criteria. Even if someone doesn’t meet every criterion, I still look at which symptoms are most impactful and how we can help.”**

Patients walk away with clarity — not labels.

### 5. A Safe, Shame-Free Experience for Patients

Many people fear they’ll appear disorganized or “all over the place” in their first appointment.

Dr. Qureshi wants them to know:

> **“Don’t be scared. It’s my job to organize their health — they don’t have to be perfectly coherent.”**

Validation is part of the therapeutic process.
