### **The Mental Strain of Balancing Work and Family**

Working women, whether mothers or not, often carry an invisible emotional and mental load. This can include:

- Meeting work deadlines while managing school drop-offs.
- Feeling pressure to “do it all” at home and in the workplace.
- Experiencing guilt for spending time at work instead of with family.
- Struggling to maintain friendships and personal interests.
- Facing burnout from the expectation of being “**always on.**“

While some women find joy and fulfillment in their careers, others feel overwhelmed by **unrealistic societal expectations**—making it difficult to prioritize mental health.

### **Common Mental Health Challenges for Working Women**

Balancing work and home life can lead to mental health struggles that often go unnoticed, including:

- **Anxiety** – Constant worry about being “good enough” at work and home.
- **Depression** – Feeling emotionally drained, disconnected, or unfulfilled.
- **Burnout** – Physical and emotional exhaustion from overcommitment.
- **Mom Guilt & Work Guilt** – Feeling like no matter where you are, you should be somewhere else.
- **Relationship Struggles** – Difficulty maintaining a healthy marriage or friendships due to lack of time.

While these struggles are common, they shouldn’t be ignored. Seeking support from a **female psychiatrist in Seattle** can make a meaningful difference.
