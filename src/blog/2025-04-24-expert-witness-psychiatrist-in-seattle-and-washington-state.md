Forensic Reports for Legal and Professional Contexts

Dr. Badeea Qureshi provides expert psychiatric opinions in select legal and administrative cases, including matters related to trauma, PTSD, and mental health evaluations. With clinical experience in treating adults and children and a background in high-quality, evidence-based care, she is available as an expert witness psychiatrist for attorneys and their clients, law firms, organizations, and courts across Washington State.

## Services Offered

Dr. Qureshi provides psychiatric consultation and written opinions in the following contexts:

- Expert witness reports for civil cases
- Mental health evaluations in cases involving PTSD, sexual abuse, trauma or other mental health related concerns
- Independent psychiatric opinions for attorneys
- Second opinions on psychiatric diagnosis and treatment
- Consultation for disability and capacity concerns

She offers services as a treating psychiatrist and consulting expert.

## Qualifications

- Residency trained at the University of Washington, Department of Behavioral Sciences
- Licensed adult psychiatrist in Washington State
- Experience serving as an expert in cases involving child custody, PTSD and physical / sexual abuse
- Clinical expertise in trauma-informed care and psychiatric diagnosis
- Thoughtful, ethical, and professional approach to documentation and testimony

Dr Qureshi accepts cases on a selective basis based on alignment with her expertise and clinical scope.

## Expert Opinion Fee Structure

- 15-minute screening call to assess scope (free)
- Initial Case Review and Consultation: Fees dependent on case complexity, fees will be as agreed in the 15-minute call
- Additional hourly rates apply for report writing, document review, depositions, and testimony
- A retainer may be required depending on case complexity

All expert services are billed privately. No insurance is accepted for medico-legal work.

## Legal Disclaimer

Dr. Qureshi’s participation as an expert witness or consultant does not establish a therapeutic relationship. All services rendered in a forensic or legal context are independent of her clinical work and are subject to a separate agreement and scope of work.
